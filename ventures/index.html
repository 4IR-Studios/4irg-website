How it works

An SPV for your round, funded by our investors.

We do not make you wait for a fund to close. When we back a company, we form a vehicle for it and bring our investors to that specific opportunity.

  1. 01

    We select

    Real meetings, not a data room crawl. We decide against the criteria below, and we tell you either way rather than going quiet.

  2. 02

    We form the SPV

    A special purpose vehicle for your company and your round. One line on your cap table, however many investors sit behind it.

  3. 03

    Our investors allocate

    We match the opportunity to the investors whose thesis it actually fits, and they allocate through the platform. You raise; we handle the mechanics.

What we have learned

Software maths does not survive contact with a factory floor.

Physical things take longer and cost more. We price that in rather than discovering it at the second raise.

What we look for

Who gets in.

We say no to most of what we see, and we would rather you knew the bar before you spend an evening on a deck.

  • You have built something physical

    A machine, a line, a plant, a grid asset — something that exists outside a slide. We are not the right fund for pre-prototype software.

  • Revenue, or a pilot with a real counterparty

    A signed pilot with an operator who has a budget counts. A letter of intent from a friendly does not.

  • A founder who has shipped in the physical world

    Someone on the team has taken hardware from drawing to installed and working, and knows what that actually costs in months.

  • A capital plan that survives a hardware timeline

    Physical things take longer and cost more than software. We look for a plan that already assumes that, rather than one that hopes otherwise.
